☀️ Solar-Powered Battery Charging System

Project Overview

The Solar-Powered Battery Charging System is an electronics project designed to convert solar energy into electrical energy and store it in a rechargeable lithium-ion battery.

The system uses photovoltaic technology, a solar charging controller and a DC-DC boost converter to provide a regulated 5V DC output for low-power applications.

🎯 Objectives

- Convert sunlight into electrical energy using a solar panel.
- Manage battery charging through a compatible charging controller.
- Store energy in a rechargeable lithium-ion battery.
- Generate a regulated 5V DC output.

🔧 Components Used

- 6V Solar Panel
- CN3065 Solar Charging Controller
- 3.7V Protected Lithium-Ion Battery
- DC-DC Boost Converter
- USB Output Connector
- Connecting Wires
- Digital Multimeter

⚙️ Working Principle

1. The solar panel converts sunlight into DC electricity.
2. The charging controller manages the battery charging process.
3. The rechargeable battery stores the generated energy.
4. The boost converter regulates the battery output to 5V DC.
5. The USB output supplies power to compatible low-power devices.

📊 Applications

- Portable solar charging systems
- Renewable energy demonstrations
- Low-power electronic devices
- Off-grid energy applications

🚀 Future Enhancements

- Arduino-based voltage and current monitoring
- LCD display for charging status
- Automatic solar tracking
- Battery percentage monitoring
